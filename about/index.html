ABOUT

Why Worth Keeping

The internet remembers everything and values almost none of it. Worth Keeping is the opposite — a small, deliberate archive of the stories, objects and ideas that continue to shape how we live, and deserve to outlast the scroll.

OUR BELIEF

Slow Down.
Look Closer.
Keep What Lasts.

Most of what shaped the modern world didn't arrive with a fanfare. It was a chance discovery, a small ritual, an object made a little too well to throw away. Worth Keeping exists to notice those things — and to hold on to them before they quietly disappear.

HOW WE CURATE

Researched.
Written.
Worth Your Time.

Every entry is drawn from the historical record and written to be read, not skimmed. Nothing is padded, nothing is chased for clicks. The test is simple: if it isn't true, and it isn't worth remembering, it doesn't go in.
